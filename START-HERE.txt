Retro Adhoc 1.2
Game Boy Advance, Game Boy and Game Boy Color for PS Vita

Thanks for trying Retro Adhoc! Here's everything you need to get playing.


What You Need
- A PS Vita with HENkaku or Enso, and VitaShell
- Your own .gba, .gb or .gbc games
- No BIOS needed: an open one is built in


Easy Install
Open the CBOC Vita Installer on your computer and drop RetroAdhoc.vpk (or this
whole ZIP) onto it. Add your games if you like. It finds your Vita and does
the rest.
https://github.com/CBOC-Vita-Lab/vita-installer/releases


Manual Install
1. Copy RetroAdhoc.vpk to your Vita with VitaShell (USB or FTP) and install it.
2. Start Retro Adhoc once. It creates ux0:data/RetroAdhoc/
3. Put your games in ux0:data/RetroAdhoc/roms/
   (games already in ux0:ROMS/ or RetroFlow's folders are found by themselves)
4. Start Retro Adhoc again and pick a game.


Updating
Just install the new VPK over the old one. Your games, saves, states and
settings stay where they are.


Saves From Another Emulator
Put the save next to the game (Game.srm, or Game.sav for a Game Boy game) or
in ux0:data/RetroAdhoc/import/. Retro Adhoc takes it in the first time you start
that game, as long as the game has no save here yet. Details: INSTALL.md.
